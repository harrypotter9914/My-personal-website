The decisions behind the game.

01

One body, a consistent rule set

Small, Medium, and Heavy change collision, speed, and load. Movement and rest alter mass, while food gives players a deliberate way to prepare the next action.

02

Teach, combine, then change the context

Counterweights, lifts, springs, optical relays, and rail docks reuse the same body-state language. Greenhouse, Foundry, and Frozen Observatory gradually recombine those rules.

03

Make retries part of the design

Checkpoints retain solved relay and dock progress. Camera revisions remove look-ahead rebound, and retry placement protects the clearance needed to attempt a puzzle again.
