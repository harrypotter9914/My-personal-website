The decisions behind the game.

01

Stage a scene that can be read

A head-on collision, a crossing sports car, and a turning truck each become a spatial investigation. Seven clues connect object placement to an explanation of what happened.

02

Make understanding compete with urgency

Investigation and ten rescue opportunities share limited time. Three difficulty settings change the timer and rescue costs; persistent Spirit connects decisions across the journey.

03

Guide attention without giving away the answer

Contextual prompts, delayed hints, inspectable journal objects, and a change from driving to small-scale exploration support discovery. Avoiding a crash is also a valid, rewarded outcome.
