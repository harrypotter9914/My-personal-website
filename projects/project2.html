The decisions behind the game.

01

A world that opens with ability

Shockwave, Double Jump, Wall Jump, and Crystal Dash change which routes the player can reach. Return paths and visible barriers connect new abilities to familiar spaces.

02

One resource, competing needs

Healing, Crystal Dash, and Shockwave share Spirit. Rune loadouts add build choices, while boss wind-ups and recovery windows make positioning part of the combat decision.

03

Revise the rule, then verify it

I clarified breakable barriers and changed Crystal Dash to charge its cost on launch. The portfolio records 38 targeted checks, and separates those checks from future player testing.
