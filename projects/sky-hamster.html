The decisions behind the game.

01

Make the device part of the game

Tilt applies acceleration rather than a fixed speed. Neutral input preserves momentum; counter-tilt brakes. Recenter and two steering mappings help players find a comfortable grip.

02

Separate balancing from machinery

Dock slowly to lock the ball, then crank to rotate a bridge, raise a lift, or extend a crossing. Release with B. Lift the device or press A to jump, carrying momentum into the landing.

03

Build courses around recovery

Short teaching courses grow into four cumulative expeditions. Playtest feedback led to stronger opposing braking while retaining acceleration, making correction more responsive without losing the momentum challenge.
