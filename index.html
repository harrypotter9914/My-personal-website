How I work

Design decisions,
made playable.

I work across gameplay and level design, prototyping, scripting, and implementation. These three projects show how I connect a mechanic to a space, teach it through play, and revise the details that get in the player’s way.

My broader experience includes interactive prototyping at Showonder, tracked-hand VR, software projects, photography, and filmmaking. Each case study separates my contribution from third-party and AI-assisted assets.
